Projekt Imitator

MACHINE LEARNING DATA PIPELINES JSONL

Projekt Imitator is a data engineering and machine learning initiative focused on dataset curation for Large Language Model fine-tuning.

Data Processing

The core of the project involves parsing massive troves of RAW and TXT data, cleaning it, and structuring it into rigorous JSONL formats. This data pipeline is the crucial first step in behavioral cloning and supervised fine-tuning (SFT).

The Goal

By curating high-quality conversational and instructional datasets, "Imitator" serves as the foundational layer for training custom AI models that can accurately replicate specific tones, knowledge bases, and interaction styles.
