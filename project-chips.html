Chips@School

PCB DESIGN KICAD EDUCATION

The Chips@School initiative revolves around demystifying printed circuit board design and semiconductor basics.

KiCad Engineering

I took the lead in designing custom PCBs in KiCad. The focus was on creating robust, easy-to-understand schematics and layout designs that can be used for educational purposes without overwhelming the user.

The Hardware

By routing the components intuitively and leaving ample room for silkscreen documentation right on the board itself, the final hardware serves not just as a functional circuit, but as a teaching tool.
